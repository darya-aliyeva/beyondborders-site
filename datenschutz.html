This is a sample page Beyond Borders is a fictional dance academy, designed for a portfolio. The company, names, address, register numbers and contacts below are invented. The structure follows what a real dance school in Germany would need.

In short

Most of our students are 12 to 17, so we keep data collection to the minimum. In plain words:

  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising trackers on this website.
  • Fonts are loaded from our own server, not from Google.
  • We only learn your phone number when you sign up for a trial class or write to us.
  • We never sell your data or pass it on for advertising.

Below are the details required by the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, the server automatically records technical data:

  • IP address, date and time of the request;
  • the page you requested and the website you came from;
  • browser type, operating system and language.

This is needed to deliver the website and protect it from attacks. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). GitHub may process this data in the USA; the transfer is covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the standard contractual clauses of the EU Commission. Details: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Cookies, fonts, tracking

Cookies

This website does not set cookies and does not use local storage. That is why you do not see a cookie banner.

Fonts

All fonts (Bebas Neue, Inter, Space Mono, Permanent Marker) are stored on our own server. Your browser does not connect to Google or any other font service.

Analytics and social media

We do not use analytics tools, tracking pixels or embedded social media widgets. The Instagram, TikTok and YouTube links in the footer are plain links: the platforms only receive data once you click and leave our website.

Trial class sign-up

The quiz and the sign-up form on our main page help us pick a suitable group and book the free trial class. We process:

  • your answers to the quiz: what your child enjoys, the age group and the time that suits you;
  • your phone number and the contact channel you prefer.

Purpose: to contact you, agree on a date and send a reminder before the class. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a contract (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR) and your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR); see Einwilligung for the full consent text.

If you do not become a member, we delete the data 6 months after the trial class. If you sign a membership, the data becomes part of the membership file (see below).

On this demo website, the forms send nothing: no data leaves your browser.

WhatsApp, Telegram, phone

Many parents prefer to message us. If you contact us via WhatsApp (WhatsApp Ireland Ltd., part of Meta) or Telegram (Telegram Messenger Inc.), the provider also processes your phone number, metadata and messages under its own privacy policy, partly outside the EU.

We use messengers only to answer you and to share class reminders, never for advertising. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR and, for the choice of channel, your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR). If you would rather avoid messengers, call us or send an email: you will get the same answer.

Students and parents

Membership

For members, we keep the contract data (names, date of birth, contacts, chosen plan, payments) and attendance lists. We need them to run classes and for accounting. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) and (c) GDPR. We keep accounting records for 10 years, as required by German tax law (§ 147 AO); everything else is deleted 12 months after membership ends.

Minors

Contracts for students under 18 are signed by a parent or guardian. Teens under 16 cannot sign up or give consent on their own: a parent does it for them (Art. 8 GDPR).

Health information

If you tell us about an injury or health condition so the coach can adjust the warm-up, we store it only with your explicit consent (Art. 9 (2) (a) GDPR), share it only with your child’s coaches and delete it when membership ends.

Photos and videos

We film classes and events for our social media only with separate written consent from the parents (and the teen, from age 14). You can say no or withdraw consent at any time: it does not affect your child’s place in the group.

Your rights

You, and your child through you, have the right to:

  • get a copy of the data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR);
  • have wrong data corrected (Art. 16) or deleted (Art. 17);
  • restrict processing (Art. 18) and receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20);
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21);
  • withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7 (3)).

One email to hallo@beyondborders.example is enough. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one responsible for us:

Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (LDI NRW)
Kavalleriestraße 2–4, 40213 Düsseldorf

Security and changes

This website uses TLS encryption: you can see it by the “https://” and the lock icon in the address bar. We update this policy when our services or the law change; the date at the top of the contents shows the current version.
