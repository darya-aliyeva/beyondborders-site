This is a sample page Beyond Borders is a fictional dance academy, designed for a portfolio. The company, names, address, register numbers and contacts below are invented. The structure follows what a real dance school in Germany would need.

Consent text

When you tick “I agree to the processing of my personal data” in the quiz or the sign-up form, you agree to the following:

I agree that Beyond Borders Tanzakademie GmbH, Grenzenlosstraße 12, 40210 Düsseldorf, processes the data I entered in this form to contact me through the channel I chose, book a free trial class and remind me of it. I am the parent or guardian of the student, or I am 16 or older. I can withdraw this consent at any time with effect for the future.

Consent is voluntary. If you do not want to fill in the form, call us at +49 211 0123 4567: we will book the trial class by phone.

What data

  • your phone number;
  • the preferred channel: WhatsApp, Telegram or phone call;
  • quiz answers: what your child enjoys, the age group and the time that suits you.

We do not ask for names, dates of birth or addresses before the trial class.

What we use it for

Only to arrange the free trial class: we call or message you within one working day, suggest a group and a time, and send a reminder the day before. No newsletters, no advertising, no sharing with partners.

If you choose WhatsApp or Telegram, the messenger provider also sees your number and messages; see Datenschutz.

Who can agree

The form is meant for parents and guardians. Teens aged 16 and 17 may fill it in themselves. For teens under 16, consent must come from a parent or guardian (Art. 8 GDPR). If we notice that a younger teen filled in the form alone, we contact the parents before booking anything.

How long we keep it

Until the trial class and up to 6 months after it, so that you can come back to the same group. Then we delete the data, unless you sign a membership. If you withdraw consent earlier, we delete it right away.

Withdrawing consent

Any time, without giving reasons and in any form: an email to hallo@beyondborders.example, a message in the chat or a word at the reception. Withdrawal applies to the future; processing before it remains lawful (Art. 7 (3) GDPR).

Photos and videos

This consent does not cover photos or videos. We ask for that separately, in writing, at the studio, and only if you want your child to appear on our social media. Saying no changes nothing about classes or events.
