Questions from parents

My child has never danced. Will they be the only beginner?

Half of our students came with no experience. In every group the coach explains the basics from the first class, so a beginner catches up in 3-4 classes

How do I know which style suits them?

Take the 3-question quiz and we'll suggest 2 groups. Or come to Open Day and try every style in one day

Can I watch a class?

Yes. Once a month there's an open class that parents watch from the hall. You can also stay during the trial class

What should they bring to the first class?

Comfortable sportswear, clean sneakers and a bottle of water. The studio has changing rooms and showers

Can we pause the membership?

Yes, for school holidays and illness. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll move the classes

Is there a discount for siblings?

The second child gets 20% off. Students aged 15-17 with a school ID also get 20% off. Discounts can't be combined

Is dance a good fit for boys?

A third of our students are boys. They often choose break dance, popping and hip-hop: lots of power and tricks
